Overview

Rclone Pribadi is a personal, non-commercial tool. It is the OAuth client used by the owner's own installation of the open-source program rclone to access the owner's own Google Drive account (and, if the owner chooses, the Google Drive accounts of a few people known personally to the owner who have explicitly granted access). This policy explains how Google user data is accessed, used, stored and shared.

Data we access

When you sign in with Google and grant permission, the app can access:

  • your Google Drive files and folders, including their content, so that they can be listed, downloaded, uploaded, modified or deleted as you instruct;
  • file metadata such as names, sizes, folder structure and modification times;
  • the access token and refresh token that Google issues so that the app can continue to act on your behalf.

The app does not access your email, contacts, calendar or any other Google service.

How the data is used

Google user data is used only to perform the file operations you request (for example copying, backing up or synchronizing files between your computer and your Google Drive). It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or for developing or training any machine-learning or AI models.

How the data is stored

The app runs entirely on the user's own computer. File contents are transferred directly between that computer and Google Drive. The access token and refresh token are stored in the rclone configuration file on that same computer. No data is sent to, or stored on, any server operated by the developer of this app.

Sharing and disclosure

Google user data is not sold, rented, transferred or disclosed to any third party. It is not shared with anyone except Google itself, as part of the normal operation of Google Drive.

Retention and deletion

Tokens remain in the local rclone configuration file until they are deleted. You can stop the app from accessing your Google account at any time in two ways: revoke its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and/or delete the corresponding remote from the rclone configuration. Files already stored in Google Drive remain under your control in Google Drive.

Compliance with Google policies

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
